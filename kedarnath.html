Kedarnath Travel Guide: What to Prepare Before You Go

Kedarnath is a high-altitude pilgrimage destination. Planning your travel, clothing, physical preparation and overnight stay in advance can help you make better decisions on the journey.

Conditions around Kedarnath can change with the season. The trek and surrounding roads require preparation, and services may be limited during busy periods or poor weather. Confirm the latest local guidance before setting out.

Check current travel information

Confirm the temple schedule, registration requirements, trek status, weather forecast and road advisories using official sources. Avoid assuming that last year's timings or arrangements are still valid.

Prepare for the walk

  • Use comfortable walking shoes that you have already tested.
  • Build up walking fitness before the trip, within your ability.
  • Carry water, snacks, rain protection and warm layers.
  • Take regular breaks and do not ignore signs of illness.

Choose where to stay

Compare the location of your accommodation with your transport plan and the time you need to depart. Ask about hot water, meals, heating, luggage storage, check-in timing and cancellation conditions. During peak dates, confirm the booking directly with the property.

For more options, visit the SpottyGo Char Dham stays page.

Pack essentials

Carry identification, any required registration details, personal medicines, a power bank, a torch and some cash. Keep essential documents available offline in case mobile connectivity is weak.

Travel safelyIf you develop concerning symptoms at altitude or feel unable to continue safely, stop and seek help from local authorities or medical staff.
