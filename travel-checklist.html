TRIP PREPARATION

India Travel Checklist: Before You Leave

Use this checklist to organise the essentials before a road trip, city break or pilgrimage. Adjust it for your destination, trip length and personal needs.

Bookings and documents

  • ☐ Confirm transport tickets and accommodation reservations.
  • ☐ Carry valid identification and any required permits or registrations.
  • ☐ Save confirmation messages, addresses and contact numbers offline.
  • ☐ Share your itinerary with a trusted contact.

Clothing and luggage

  • ☐ Pack clothes suited to the destination and forecast.
  • ☐ Bring comfortable shoes and rain protection when appropriate.
  • ☐ Use a small day bag for water, documents and daily essentials.
  • ☐ Keep valuables and important medicines in your hand luggage.

Health and safety

  • ☐ Pack personal medicines and basic first-aid supplies.
  • ☐ Check whether your destination needs special preparation.
  • ☐ Review weather, road conditions and official travel notices.
  • ☐ Save local emergency contacts and know where to ask for help.

Money and connectivity

  • ☐ Carry a suitable mix of payment methods and some cash.
  • ☐ Charge your phone and pack a power bank.
  • ☐ Download maps or tickets you may need offline.
  • ☐ Check mobile network coverage for remote areas.

For pilgrimage trips

Confirm temple schedules, registration requirements, local rules and transport arrangements with official sources. Pack modest, comfortable clothing appropriate for the location and season. For mountain trips, allow time for rest and possible delays.

Final checkReconfirm key bookings and travel conditions one day before departure. Keep your plan flexible when weather or road conditions may change.
