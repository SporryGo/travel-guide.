About Khatu Bagh Resort

A practical accommodation option to consider when planning your Khatu Shyam Ji darshan, family visit or group pilgrimage.

Khatu Bagh Resort is located on Ringas Road in Khatu, Sikar, Rajasthan. Its location makes it worth considering for travellers who want to stay in the Khatu area while visiting the famous Khatu Shyam Ji Temple.

The property listing describes air-conditioned rooms, hot water, attached bathrooms, parking, CCTV cameras and family-room options. Guests can also check the availability of chargeable meals and other facilities before booking.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends or a group of devotees, compare the room capacity, facilities, total price and property rules before confirming your stay.
